How to play

Tap a compartment and its whole front row hops out at once. Each animal jumps into a crate that already holds its kind, or claims an empty one. Three of a kind ships the crate off. Animals with no place to go wait in the row below, and five waiting animals means trouble. Boosters such as Shuffle, Undo and Instant Fill help in tight spots.

FAQ

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. The game works offline and needs no sign-in.

Does the game collect my data?
Only limited technical and usage data through Unity analytics. No ads. See the Privacy Policy.
