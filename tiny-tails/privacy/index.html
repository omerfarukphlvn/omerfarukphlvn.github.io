Tiny Tails: Zoo Express
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HopeForge handles information in Tiny Tails: Zoo Express (the Game), also known during development as ZooSort.

Game data on your device

The Game does not require an account or sign-in. Progress, coins, booster inventory and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account or cloud-save server for this version. Your operating system may include app data in its backups, according to your device and cloud-backup settings.

Technical information and Unity

The Game is made with Unity and has Unity's built-in analytics enabled. Unity may process technical and usage information such as device and installation identifiers, IP address, device model, operating system, app version and session or app-use information. This information supports usage measurement and operation of Unity services. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for its processing, retention and privacy choices.

This version does not use Firebase, GameAnalytics or Meta analytics integrations. It does not display advertisements or offer real-money in-app purchases. If those features or new data processing are introduced, we will update this policy before releasing the change.

Support and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to attach. We use this information to answer your request and investigate issues. Please avoid sending sensitive information. We do not sell your personal information. Support messages are handled through our email provider, Google. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

The app store handles downloads and any store-level diagnostics under its own policies: Google and Apple. These providers, Unity, and our email provider may process information in countries other than your own.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local progress remains until app data is cleared or removed; device backups may retain a separate copy. On Android you can clear the Game's storage in device settings. Removing the app may remove local progress; manage cloud backups separately. We cannot restore a local save that has been deleted.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion of information held by HopeForge by emailing us. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location. For information held by Unity or an app store, their linked policies describe the available controls and request channels.

Children and privacy

The Game has no chat, public profiles or account-registration form. Parents or guardians who believe a child has sent personal information to HopeForge can contact us to request its deletion. Please do not send a child's personal details in a support request unless necessary to resolve it.

This website

These policy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies to these pages.

Updates and contact

We will publish revisions here with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact HopeForge at omerphlvn1@gmail.com.
