Pickaxe Rush: Digger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Pickaxe Rush: Digger (the "Game"), published by HopeForge, handles your information.

1. Data we collect

The Game has no account, no sign-in and no ads. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's built-in services automatically collect a limited amount of technical data:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as a random installation ID generated by Unity.
  • App interactions, such as when the Game is started and how long a session lasts.
  • Diagnostics, such as device model, operating system version and performance information.

This data is used only for analytics: to understand how the Game is used and to keep it running well. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Collection is required for the Game to run and cannot be turned off in the Game.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, levels and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the Game removes this data.

3. Third parties

The technical data in section 1 is processed by Unity Technologies as our service provider, under the Unity Privacy Policy. We do not share your data with any other third party. Downloads and updates are handled by the app store you use, under the Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Your choices

You can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the Game. For questions about Unity's data processing, or to request access to or deletion of data, contact us at the address below or see the Unity Privacy Policy.

6. Changes

If the Game starts collecting other data (for example, to show ads), we will update this policy before that version is released. The current version is always published on this page.
