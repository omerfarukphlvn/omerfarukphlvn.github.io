Pickaxe Rush: Digger Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Pickaxe Rush: Digger (the "Game"), published by HopeForge, handles your information.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The Game has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, levels and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the Game removes this data.

3. Third parties

The Game does not share data with third parties because it does not collect any. Downloads and updates are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

5. Changes

If a future version of the Game starts collecting data (for example, to show ads), we will update this policy before that version is released. The current version is always published on this page.
