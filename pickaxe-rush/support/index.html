How to play

Press and hold to aim, release to drop your pickaxe. Pickaxes break blocks and earn coins. Pass through laser gates to multiply your pickaxes, then spend coins on upgrades between rounds to dig deeper.

FAQ

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

Do I need an account or internet connection?
No. The game works offline and needs no sign-in.

Does the game collect my data?
Only limited technical data through the Unity engine, used for analytics. No personal information and no ads. See the Privacy Policy.
